# Code of conduct

Nova follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/YoussefSah05/nova/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even for conduct reports; it is the project's private channel). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove content and restrict participation, following the
Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
